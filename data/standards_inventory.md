## Decision Framework for Standards Applicability

Before applying any standard, the Risk & Compliance Agent runs a 3-layer trigger analysis:

```
STEP 1: Subject matter → is this a regulated activity?
STEP 2: Explicit invocation → does the contract name the standard?
STEP 3a: Subject matter test → does the contract involve the regulated subject?
STEP 3b: Jurisdictional nexus → is there a territorial hook?
STEP 3c: Materiality → is the regulated thing actually present?

ONLY if ALL three Step 3 sub-tests pass → standard applies
```

General contract law always applies as the baseline, determined by governing law jurisdiction:
- US/Delaware/state-law governed contracts: US_RESTATEMENT + US_UCC (if sale of goods) + US_DGCL (if Delaware entity)
- Indian-law governed contracts: Indian Contract Act, 1872 + IT Act, 2000
- Other jurisdictions: flag limitation - do not fabricate

Retrieval is semantic (SentenceTransformer embeddings + FAISS inner-product search), not keyword-based. Applicability gating is done by the LLM using the framework above - the LLM must positively justify every standard it cites and explicitly list standards it reviewed but excluded.
